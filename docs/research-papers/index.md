# Research Papers

Analyses commissioned for CBWeb3, covering the three areas that most affect the design of
a multi-jurisdiction tokenized central bank money network.

| Paper | Topic |
|---|---|
| [Governance Analysis for CBWeb3](Governance_Analysis_for_CBWeb3.pdf) | Governance models for a shared regional settlement network, decision rights, and the role of the participating central banks |
| [Interoperability Analysis for CBWeb3](Interoperability_Analysis_for_CBWeb3.pdf) | Cross-network settlement approaches, standards, and the Hub & Spoke interoperability model |
| [Privacy Analysis for CBWeb3](Privacy_Analysis_for_CBWeb3.pdf) | On-chain privacy techniques, confidentiality requirements of supervised institutions, and their regulatory implications |

See also the [Privacy vs Confidentiality](../dpg-assesment/Privacy%20vs%20Confidentiality.md)
analysis, which develops the distinction these papers rely on.
